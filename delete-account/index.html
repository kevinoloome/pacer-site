Delete your account

You can delete your TwendeRun account yourself, any time.

In the app

  1. Open TwendeRun and sign in (guests: open the app on the same phone).
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile, including your name and pace range.
  • The runs you joined, your chat messages, your stories, and your blocks and reports.
  • Upcoming runs you host. Past runs you hosted stay for the people who ran them, shown as hosted by “Deleted user”.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Can’t get into the app?

Email support@twenderun.com from the email you signed up with, or tell us the name you used, and we will delete your account.

See also our Privacy Policy.
