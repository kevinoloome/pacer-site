TwendeRun.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

TwendeRun helps runners in Kenya find running partners and join runs. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why, who can see it and what choices you have. By using the TwendeRun website or app you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

TwendeRun ("we", "us") is a running-partner service based in Nairobi, Kenya. In this policy, "the app" means the TwendeRun web app and any Android app made from it, and "the website" means twenderun.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name (or the display name you choose), and, if you save an account, your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are stored in a protected, scrambled form, not as plain text.
  • Guest accounts: if you use the app without signing up, we create a guest account for you with an automatic name such as "Runner 123" and a random identifier.
  • Runs you post: the meeting place, town, date and time, distance, pace, surface, note, and the name shown as host.
  • Runs you join and the people who joined a run.
  • Chat messages you send in a run's chat, and stories you share about a run.
  • Reports, blocks and feedback: when you report a run or story, block someone, or send us feedback (including an optional contact detail you choose to add).

Information stored on your device

  • Small settings in your browser or app, such as your sign-in session, your usual pace range, your home town, and whether you have seen the welcome screens or dismissed a reminder.

Information collected automatically

  • Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the service running and secure.
  • We record which chats you have read, so we can show you a "new messages" marker.

What we do not collect: we do not read your phone's GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone. A meeting place is only the text a host types. When you tap "Open in Google Maps", Google Maps opens separately and handles your location under its own policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To run the service: show runs, let you join, chat and share stories.
  • To show you runs that match your pace and place.
  • To keep people safe: handle reports and blocks, remove harmful content, and stop spam or abuse.
  • To improve TwendeRun, for example by reading the feedback you send.
  • To contact you about your account or a run you joined, if you have given us an email address.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show advertising.

4. Who can see what

  • Visible to other users of the app: runs you post (with your host name), your name in a run's "Who's going" list, and stories you share (for 14 days).
  • Visible only to people in a run: that run's chat. Only the host and people who joined can read it.
  • Not shown to other users: your email address, password and your reports.

Because runs are public and meet in person, please only share what you are comfortable with other runners seeing, and meet in public places.

5. Services we use

We use trusted providers to run TwendeRun. They process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase for our database, sign-in and live chat.
  • Vercel to host the website and app.
  • Google Maps, only when you tap a map link.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and other apps, only when you choose to share a run link through them.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics at the moment. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

6. Moderation and safety

Anyone can report a run or a story, and block another user. We may review the reported content, including related chat messages, to decide what to do. We may remove content or accounts that put people at risk or break the rules, such as fake runs, harassment or spam.

7. How long we keep information

  • Your account, runs, joins and chat messages are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until the run or account is removed.
  • Stories are shown for 14 days.
  • Reports and safety records may be kept for longer where needed to protect people.

8. Your choices and rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you,
  • correct information that is wrong,
  • delete your information, and
  • stop using your information in a certain way.

You can change your name in the app at any time, delete your own stories, and leave runs you have joined. To delete your account and the data linked to it, use Delete my account on the Profile tab in the app, or see how to delete your account. If you can't get into the app, email us at the address below from the email you signed up with, or tell us which guest name was yours. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya (odpc.go.ke).

9. Children

TwendeRun is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has used the app, please contact us and we will delete their information.

10. Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections and access rules so that people can only see what they are meant to. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong password and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TwendeRun grows. We will change the "Last updated" date at the top, and tell you in the app if the changes are important.

12. Contact us

Questions, requests to access or delete your information, or concerns about safety: support@twenderun.com
